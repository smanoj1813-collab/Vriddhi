THE PROBLEM

A college runs on 9 disconnected tools — and one weekend-eating ritual

Fragmented records
Attendance, fees, timetables and results live in separate spreadsheets. Every semester ends with manual re-entry and no single source of truth.
The exam-paper bottleneck
Faculty hand-build a paper for every subject, every semester — then spend weekends marking scripts. Paper-making, not teaching, consumes the cycle.
Compliance by archaeology
NAAC, the university and the state portal each demand evidence. Colleges reconstruct it from registers weeks after the fact.
Students get nothing back
No app, no placements, no visibility. The institution's own data never reaches the learner who generated it.

THE CORE

Curated + imported + AI-generated questions, tagged by topic and difficulty.
2 · Paper
Blueprint by unit, marks and difficulty. AI drafts; faculty approve.

Online test with lockdown, section breaks, autosave and expiry recovery.
4 · Grade
MCQ auto-grade; AI grading suggestions with confidence scores and rubrics.

Grade records → transcripts; university result files imported back in.
