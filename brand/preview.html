Using it

Do

  • Use the teal version on white, slate-50, or a photo behind a scrim
  • Use the reverse version on slate-900 / #0b1220 or darker
  • Use the one-colour version for restricted palettes
  • Keep a clear margin equal to the height of the “V” on every side

Don't

  • Don't recolour outside the palette above
  • Don't stretch, skew, rotate or stroke the outlines
  • Don't put the wordmark on a busy photo — use the stacked lockup
  • Don't put a hard shadow, glow or bevel on the flat logo
