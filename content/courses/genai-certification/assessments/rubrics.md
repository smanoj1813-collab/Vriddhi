# Assessment Rubrics — VGC-101 GenAI for Career Impact

This document collects every rubric used in the programme so that learners, facilitators and moderators work from the same standards. Each project brief in `projects/` repeats its own rubric; this file is the authoritative version.

## Grading scheme at a glance

| Component | Weight | Instrument | Where the rubric lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson quizzes | 15 | 35 quizzes × 5 MCQs, best attempt, averaged then scaled | Auto-scored |
| Project Blocks 1–5 | 35 (7 each) | Rubrics below (3-point scale per criterion) | §2 |
| Capstone | 30 | 6-criterion rubric | §3 |
| Final assessment | 15 | 60 MCQs + 2 scenarios | `final-assessment-blueprint.md` |
| Reflection journal | 5 | 8 module entries, holistic rubric | §4 |

**Pass:** ≥ 50 overall **and** capstone ≥ 12/30 **and** ≥ 4 of 5 project blocks submitted **and** ≥ 75% attendance in instructor-led batches. **Grades:** Distinction ≥ 80 · Merit ≥ 65 · Pass ≥ 50 · Not yet < 50.

## 1. General marking principles

1. **Evidence over polish.** Marks follow demonstrated work — logs, test sheets, screenshots, recordings — not presentation quality.
2. **Honesty is rewarded.** Stated limits, failed experiments and documented refusals earn marks; concealment loses them.
3. **AI use is expected and must be disclosed.** Every submission carries an AI-use statement. Missing statements lose the "reflection/disclosure" mark; misleading statements are misconduct.
4. **Adaptation, not reproduction.** Prompts, templates or text copied from the lessons without adaptation to the learner's context score 0 on the relevant criterion.
5. **Integrity caps.** Specific violations cap a block at 3/7 regardless of other quality (real likeness without consent; fabricated data; messaging real people during testing without a checkpoint; personal data sent to AI steps without justification; cloned voice without consent).
6. **Language equity.** Work in Kannada, Hindi or other Indian languages is assessed on the same criteria; facilitators arrange a competent reader where needed.
7. **Team work.** Team marks may be adjusted by ±20% per individual based on build logs, reflections and observed participation, with reasons recorded.
8. **Feedback within 10 days**, written against the criteria, with one specific improvement per criterion below 3.

## 2. Project Block rubrics (7 marks each)

Scale for each criterion: **0–1 = not yet · 2 = meets · 3 = strong** (criteria worth 1 or 2 marks use the top of their range for "strong").

### PB1 — Personal Prompt Playbook

| Criterion | Marks | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt quality | 3 | Vague, generic, missing audience/format. | Mostly structured; a few missing blocks. | Every prompt precise, structured (RTF/CO-STAR), usable by a stranger. |
| Evidence & verification | 2 | No log or checks. | Log present; checks named, not always performed. | Log complete; every entry has a real check with result; ≥ 1 caught an error. |
| Coverage & usefulness | 1 | Requirements missed. | Requirements met. | Met and clearly tied to the learner's degree and goal. |
| Reflection & disclosure | 1 | Missing/generic; no AI-use statement. | Present but shallow. | Specific, honest, AI-use statement included. |

### PB2 — Multi-format Campaign Kit

| Criterion | Marks | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Craft & consistency | 3 | Unrelated assets; text errors; wrong formats. | Mostly consistent; minor fundamentals issues. | One campaign; kit applied; fundamentals sound; clean text in all languages. |
| Process & tool fluency | 2 | Thin log; single tool; no editing. | Log present; sensible tools; some editing. | Planned prompts; single-variable iteration; editing; repurposing chain. |
| Accessibility & ethics | 1 | No record or violations. | Record with gaps. | Contrast, alt text, captions, four-question routine, disclosures, rights. |
| Brief fit & reflection | 1 | Assets do not serve brief. | Brief served. | Clearly achieves purpose for audience; specific reflection. |

### PB3 — Bilingual Audio Episode

| Criterion | Marks | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audio craft | 3 | Mispronunciations, noise, uneven levels, music over voice. | Clean; minor pacing/level issues. | Studio-grade; natural pauses; ducked music; consistent voice; correct loudness. |
| Script & accuracy | 2 | Read from a document; unverified claims. | Ear-written; fact sheet mostly complete. | Re-told for the ear; every claim verified; idiomatic Indian-language version. |

| Listener test & reflection | 1 | None. | Collected, not acted on. | Both language audiences; specific changes; honest reflection. |

### PB4 — Working Automation or Mini-App

| Criterion | Marks | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| It works and is safe | 3 | Does not run; untested sends; no error handling; data exposed. | Core flow works; partial error handling/checkpoint. | End-to-end; error handler and checkpoint demonstrated; data minimised; kill switch documented. |

| Demo & reflection | 1 | Missing. | Demo shows build; generic reflection. | Problem, build, failure handled, next step; specific reflection. |

### PB5 — AI Ethics & Safety Audit

| Criterion | Marks | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence quality | 3 | Opinions; too few runs; unclear method. | Protocol followed; adequate runs. | Paired prompts, volume, coded sheet, before/after mitigation, sources opened; limits stated. |
| Coverage (FATE + privacy/security) | 2 | One dimension. | Most dimensions; some thin. | Fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy and security each with specific findings. |
| Decision & recommendations | 1 | Missing or "ban"/"all fine". | Generic. | Actionable conditions, owners, retest dates, tied to severity. |
| Clarity & honesty | 1 | Disorganised; overstated. | Clear. | Manager-ready; honest about what was not found. |

## 3. Capstone rubric (30 marks)

| Criterion | Marks | Not yet (0–40%) | Meets (41–70%) | Strong (71–100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem & validation | 6 | Solution-first; no or hypothetical interviews. | Specific user; ≥ 5 interviews; alternatives listed. | Interviews about past behaviour; honest alternatives; riskiest assumptions named and tested; canvas evolved with evidence. |
| Working product | 7 | Does not run; no backup. | Runs (or backup shown); AI feature present. | Runs end-to-end; sensible documented architecture; AI feature narrow, validated, with fallback; integration issues found and fixed. |
| Pilot evidence & iteration | 6 | No real users or numbers. | Real users; some numbers; feedback collected. | Four numbers tracked daily; synthesis from the log; ≥ 1 iteration shipped and measured; honest decision memo. |
| Responsible build | 5 | No notice; data exposed; no checkpoints. | Notice and checkpoints present. | Notice + consent; data minimised and mapped; checkpoints; kill switch; unacceptable outputs blocked; language reviewed; limits stated. |
| Pitch & report | 4 | Over time; feature list; no evidence. | On time; evidence present; demo shown. | Evidence-led; every member speaks; failure handled in demo; hard questions answered with data; traceable two-page report. |
| Teamwork & contribution | 2 | No log; contributions unclear. | Log present; contributions stated. | Daily log with credited work matching the report; specific reflections. |

**Capstone pass threshold:** 12/30. Individual adjustment ±20% with recorded reasons.

## 4. Reflection journal (5 marks, holistic)

Eight entries (one per module; learners may choose which lesson prompt to answer, minimum 120 words; the final entry 200 words).

| Mark | Descriptor |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | All 8 entries; specific to the learner's own work; names failures and changes in practice; connects modules; honest about AI use. |
| 4 | All 8 entries; mostly specific; some connections. |
| 3 | 6–7 entries or entries that are present but generic. |
| 2 | 4–5 entries. |
| 0–1 | Fewer than 4 entries, or entries evidently AI-generated without personal content. |

## 5. Moderation

- Facilitators mark; a second marker samples **20% of PBs and all capstones** (or at least 5 per cohort) blind; disagreements > 1 mark on a block or > 4 on the capstone are resolved by discussion and recorded.
- The programme team reviews mark distributions per cohort and adjusts guidance (not marks) where drift is found.
- Learners may request a review within 7 days of feedback; a different marker re-marks against the rubric.

## 6. Late and resubmission policy (summary)

- Up to 3 days late: no penalty with prior notice; otherwise −1 mark on the block.
- 4–10 days late: capped at 4/7 (capstone report: −4).
- One resubmission per block is permitted for scores below 3.5/7, within 10 days of feedback, capped at 5/7.
- Full detail in `certification-policy.md`.
