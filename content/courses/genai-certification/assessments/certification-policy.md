# Certification Policy — Certified GenAI Practitioner (Vriddhi)

Programme: **VGC-101 GenAI for Career Impact — Certificate Program** · 60 hours · 4 credits (recommended) · 10 weeks · Version 1.0 (September 2026)

## 1. Award

The certificate **Certified GenAI Practitioner (Vriddhi)** is awarded to learners who meet all of the following:

1. **Overall score ≥ 50%** across the weighted components (quizzes 15, project blocks 35, capstone 30, final assessment 15, journal 5).
2. **Capstone ≥ 12/30** (40% of its weight).
3. **At least 4 of 5 Project Blocks submitted** and marked (a submission scoring 0 for integrity reasons does not count as submitted).
4. **Attendance/activity ≥ 75%** of guided sessions for instructor-led batches; for self-paced learners, completion of all lesson quizzes and journal entries substitutes for attendance.
5. **No unresolved academic-integrity finding.**

Grade bands: **Distinction ≥ 80 · Merit ≥ 65 · Pass ≥ 50 · Not yet < 50.** The certificate states the grade band, the version of the programme and the date; a verification link or QR code points to the credential record.

## 2. Credits and recognition

- Designed as a **4-credit** skill-enhancement / value-added course (60 notional hours: 20 guided, 20 self-study, 20 lab/project) suitable for affiliated colleges' add-on course frameworks. Colleges map credits under their own regulations; the programme team provides the syllabus, hour split and assessment scheme on request.
- Hours may be recorded in the college's activity or ability-enhancement register where applicable.
- The certificate does not claim recognition by any statutory body unless stated on the certificate itself.

## 3. Academic integrity and AI use

The programme teaches professional AI use; the integrity rules follow from that.

**Expected:** using AI tools for drafting, editing, analysis, generation and rehearsal, **disclosed** through the AI-use statement on every submission, with the learner's own selection, verification, judgement and reflection.

**Misconduct:**

- Submitting AI-generated work as one's own reasoning without disclosure; false or misleading AI-use statements.
- Fabricating data, interviews, users, test runs, sources or results.
- Reproducing lesson material, other learners' work or public material without attribution.
- Prompt injection or other manipulation of assessment systems.
- Using AI tools during Part A of the final assessment; communicating with others during Part B.
- Ethical violations in project work: real likeness or cloned voice without documented consent; personal data sent to AI tools or published without justification; messaging real people during testing without a checkpoint; producing deceptive synthetic media.

**Process:** the facilitator documents the concern and evidence; the learner responds within 7 days; a panel of two (facilitator + programme lead or another facilitator) decides. **Outcomes** range from resubmission with a cap, to zero on the component, to withholding of the certificate. Decisions are recorded; learners may appeal once to the programme lead within 14 days.

## 4. Attempts, resits and resubmissions

| Situation | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Lesson quizzes | Unlimited attempts; best attempt counts. |
| Project block below 3.5/7 | One resubmission within 10 days of feedback; capped at 5/7. |
| Project block not submitted | May be submitted late per §5; if the learner has ≥ 4 blocks, a missing fifth block scores 0 without preventing certification. |
| Capstone below 12/30 | One revised submission (report, evidence pack and recorded pitch) within 21 days; capped at 18/30. |
| Final assessment below 40% | One resit within 30 days; the resit mark is capped at 60% of the component. |
| Overall below 50 after all of the above | "Not yet" — learner may re-enrol in a later cohort; project blocks and capstone marks may be carried forward for 12 months. |

## 5. Deadlines, extensions and late work

- Deadlines are published in the cohort calendar; the default rhythm is: PB1 Week 3, PB2 Week 5, PB3 Week 6, PB4 Week 7, PB5 Week 9, capstone showcase Week 9, final assessment Week 10.
- **Extensions** of up to 7 days are granted for documented illness, bereavement, exams clash or connectivity failure; request before the deadline where possible.
- **Late without extension:** ≤ 3 days −1 mark; 4–10 days capped at 4/7 (capstone report −4); > 10 days accepted for completion but scored 0 unless the programme lead approves otherwise.

## 6. Accessibility and inclusion

- Reasonable accommodations (extra time, readers/scribes, alternative formats, flexible deadlines) are provided on request with basic documentation; requests go to the facilitator or programme lead in Week 1 where possible.
- Learners may write journal entries, Part B of the final assessment and project reflections in **English, Kannada or Hindi**; other languages by arrangement.
- Free tiers of tools are sufficient for every requirement; where a tool is unavailable or blocked, the facilitator approves an equivalent. No learner is penalised for a paid feature.
- Learners without a private device or reliable connectivity should be prioritised for lab time; hands-on labs may be completed on shared devices with the facilitator's record.

## 7. Data protection for learners

- The programme collects the minimum personal data needed for enrolment, assessment and certification; progress data held in the Vriddhi app is described in the app's privacy notice.
- Project work involving other people's personal data must follow the course's DPDP-aligned checklists; facilitators may require removal or anonymisation before marking.
- Learner submissions may be used as anonymised exemplars for training facilitators only with the learner's written consent.

## 8. Quality assurance and versioning

- The course pack is versioned (`course.json` → `version`, `lastReviewed`). Cohorts are assessed against the version they enrolled on.
- Each cohort: moderation per `rubrics.md` §5; item statistics for the final assessment; learner feedback survey; a tool-landscape check and a legal-update check (DPDP stages, IT Rules on synthetic media, EU AI Act deadlines) before the next cohort.
- Material changes to assessment weights or pass conditions are announced before a cohort starts, never during it.

## 9. Certificate verification and revocation

- Certificates carry a unique ID; verification is available through the Vriddhi platform.
- A certificate may be revoked if a serious integrity finding is established after award; the learner is notified with reasons and has the right of appeal in §3.

## 10. Roles

- **Facilitator:** delivers sessions, supports labs, marks, records attendance, raises integrity concerns.
- **Second marker / moderator:** samples and moderates per `rubrics.md`.
- **Programme lead:** owns this policy, decides appeals, approves extensions beyond 7 days and equivalent-tool substitutions, maintains the held-back item bank.
- **Learner:** engages honestly, discloses AI use, protects others' data, meets deadlines or asks early.

*Questions about this policy go to the programme lead named in the cohort calendar.*
