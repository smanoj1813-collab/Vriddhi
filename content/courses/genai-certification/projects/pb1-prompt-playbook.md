# Project Block 1 — Personal Prompt Playbook

> **Project plan** · 60 minutes guided + 2–3 hours independent work · Submit by the end of Week 3 · Weight: 7% of the final grade

## The brief

Build a **Personal Prompt Playbook**: ten tested, reusable prompts for tasks in *your* degree and career goal, each with evidence that it works and a note on what to check afterwards. The playbook is the foundation of your course portfolio and the first thing you will show an employer who asks "how do you actually use AI?"

## What to submit

One document (Google Doc, Word or PDF) or a shared sheet containing:

1. **Cover page** — your name, programme, the Week-10 goal sentence from Lesson 1.1, and an **AI-use statement**.
2. **Ten playbook entries**, each with:
   - **Name** and **use when** (the trigger situation).
   - The **final one-shot prompt** (RTF or CO-STAR).
   - **Pattern(s) used** (few-shot, chain step, self-critique…).
   - **Best tool** and the evidence-log row that supports the choice.
   - **Check afterwards** — the specific verification you run on the output.
   - **Example output** (link or excerpt) with your rubric scores (Correct, Complete, Constraint-compliant, Clear, Credible).
   - **Version note** — what changed between your first attempt and the final prompt.
3. **One prompt chain** (counts as up to three of the ten entries) for a substantial task — e.g. research summary → structure → draft.
4. **At least one bilingual prompt** (Kannada/Hindi/Tamil/Telugu + English).
5. **Evidence log** — the comparison sheet from Lessons 2.1 and 2.6 (minimum eight rows).
6. **Reflection (250–300 words)** — what you learned about how the tools fail, which pattern you will rely on most, and one thing you will *not* delegate to AI.

## Coverage requirements

Your ten prompts must include at least:

- 3 **produce** prompts (draft an email, report section, caption, question bank…)
- 2 **analyse** prompts (compare, classify, evaluate, calculate with checkable steps)
- 2 **learn** prompts (explain, quiz me, viva practice)
- 1 **communicate** prompt aimed at a non-expert or community audience
- 1 **critique** prompt (persona panel or rubric self-critique)
- 1 prompt of your choice that supports your Week-10 goal

## Process (suggested)

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Week 2, session 2 | Choose your ten tasks from the Lesson 1.1 audit; draft five prompts. |
| Week 2, self-study | Run, iterate and A/B test; log evidence; apply discipline checks. |
| Week 3, session 1 | Peer review: swap playbooks with a classmate, run two of each other's prompts *without help*; note what was unclear. |
| Week 3, self-study | Finalise entries, write the reflection, submit. |

## Rubric (7 marks)

| Criterion | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Prompt quality** (3) | Vague, generic, missing audience or format. | Mostly well-structured; a few missing blocks. | Every prompt is precise, structured (RTF/CO-STAR), and a stranger could use it. |
| **Evidence & verification** (2) | No evidence log or checks; outputs accepted at face value. | Log present; checks named but not always performed. | Log complete; every entry has a real check with the result recorded; at least one caught an error. |
| **Coverage & usefulness** (1) | Requirements missed; prompts do not match the learner's real tasks. | Requirements met. | Requirements met and prompts clearly tied to the learner's degree and goal. |
| **Reflection & disclosure** (1) | Missing or generic; no AI-use statement. | Present but shallow. | Specific, honest, and includes an AI-use statement. |

**Pass threshold for this block:** 3.5 / 7. Entries copied from the lessons without adaptation score 0 for prompt quality.

## Tips from previous cohorts

- Start with tasks you *actually did* last week. Invented tasks produce playbooks nobody opens.
- The "check afterwards" field is where most marks are lost. Be specific: not "verify facts" but "confirm the GST threshold on cbic.gov.in and recompute the total in Sheets".
- Peer review is not optional politeness — a prompt a classmate cannot run is a prompt you do not understand yet.
- Keep the playbook alive after submission. You will add to it in every remaining module and present it in Module 8.

## AI-use statement template

> *AI-use statement: I used [tools] to draft and test the prompts in this playbook. Outputs quoted as examples were generated by [tool] on [dates] and checked as described in each entry. The selection of tasks, the evaluation scores, the version notes and the reflection are my own work.*
