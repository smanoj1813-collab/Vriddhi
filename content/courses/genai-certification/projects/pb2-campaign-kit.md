# Project Block 2 — Multi-format Campaign Kit

> **Project plan** · 75 minutes guided + 3–4 hours independent work · Submit by the end of Week 5 · Weight: 7% of the final grade

## The brief

Design and produce a **complete visual campaign kit** for a real or realistic client — a college club or event, a local small business, an NGO drive, a department initiative, or your own venture idea. The kit must show that you can go from brief to brand to finished assets across formats, and that you can do it responsibly.

## What to submit

A single folder (shared drive link) or PDF portfolio containing:

1. **Creative brief (1 page)** — purpose, audience, message, formats, must-haves, constraints, success measure.
2. **Brand sheet (1 page)** — colours with hex codes, fonts, logo usage, photo/illustration style, caption voice; your **image base prompt** and **caption base prompt**.
3. **Campaign assets (minimum eight, across at least five formats):**
   - 1 poster (A3 or A4) — bilingual (English + Kannada/Hindi/Tamil/Telugu)
   - 3 social posts (1:1 or 4:5) forming a consistent set
   - 1 story/reel-format static (9:16)
   - 1 carousel (minimum 5 slides)
   - 1 short video (30–60 s) with voice, burned-in captions and music — *avatar optional*
   - 1 infographic or data visual built from real numbers
   - Optional: WhatsApp broadcast image, email header, quote cards
4. **Process log** — for each asset: tool(s), key prompts, number of generations, edits performed, time taken.
5. **Accessibility and ethics record** — contrast checks, alt text for every image, caption proofreading evidence, the four-question ethics routine answered per asset, disclosures/labels applied, rights record (music, fonts, images, likenesses).
6. **Reflection (300 words)** — what the tools did well, where they failed, what you did by hand, and how long a professional would take with and without AI.
7. **AI-use statement** on the cover.

## Constraints

- No real person's likeness unless you have documented consent (attach it).
- No trademarked characters, logos or artwork you do not own.
- Every chart or statistic must come from a real source you cite.
- All text in every language proofread by a competent reader (name them in the record).
- Assets must be exported at platform-correct sizes.

## Process (suggested)

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Week 4, session 1 | Choose the client; write the brief; get a classmate to challenge it. |
| Week 4, session 2 | Brand sheet and base prompts; generate image set; poster and posts. |
| Week 4, self-study | Video: script, storyboard, clips, assembly. Infographic. |
| Week 5, session 1 | Peer review in pairs using the rubric; fix; accessibility and ethics record. |
| Week 5, self-study | Reflection; final export; submit. |

## Rubric (7 marks)

| Criterion | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Craft and consistency** (3) | Assets look unrelated; text errors; wrong formats; generator text left in. | Mostly consistent; minor fundamentals problems (hierarchy, contrast, alignment). | Clearly one campaign; kit applied throughout; fundamentals sound; text clean in all languages. |
| **Process and tool fluency** (2) | Process log missing or thin; single tool; no editing. | Log present; sensible tool choices; some editing. | Log shows planned prompts, iteration by single variables, editing (inpaint/expand/cutout), repurposing chain. |
| **Accessibility and ethics** (1) | No record, or violations (likeness, copyright, invented data). | Record present with gaps. | Full record: contrast, alt text, captions, four-question routine, disclosures, rights. |
| **Brief fit and reflection** (1) | Assets do not serve the brief; reflection generic. | Brief served; reflection adequate. | Assets clearly achieve the brief's purpose for its audience; reflection specific and honest. |

**Pass threshold for this block:** 3.5 / 7. Any asset that uses a real person's likeness without consent or fabricated data fails the ethics criterion and caps the block at 3.

## Tips from previous cohorts

- The strongest kits picked a *small* client with a *real* event date. Constraints produce better work.
- Do the video early — it takes longer than everything else combined.
- If your Kannada or Hindi poster has a typo, the campaign has a typo. Get a native reader to check before you export.
- Keep the process log as you go, not afterwards; markers can tell.
