# Project Block 2 — 30-Day Content Engine

> **Project plan** · 60 minutes guided + 3–4 hours independent work · Submit by the end of Week 4 · Weight: 7% of the final grade

## The brief

Build and run a **30-day content engine** for your lab business: a strategy-backed calendar, one AI-assisted piece actually published, the verification trail behind it, and the repurposing + distribution machinery that gives it a second life. This block proves the **content half** of the Digital Marketing Executive role: you can plan it, produce it safely with AI, and distribute it deliberately.

Publishing for real is a requirement — a "hypothetical" post that never met an audience teaches nothing about distribution or feedback.

## What to submit

One document or shared folder containing:

1. **Cover** — business, dates, your name, and an **AI-use statement** (what AI drafted, what you verified, what you changed).
2. **One-line strategy** (Lesson 3.1) — cadence, pillars, persona, KPI.
3. **Pillar/cluster map** — three pillars with their clusters, each cluster item traced to a keyword, persona barrier or review gap.
4. **The 30-day calendar** — full columns (date, pillar, topic, format, funnel stage, owner, status, distribution), planned at ≤50% capacity, with your cut order marked.
5. **The published piece** — the AI-assisted article or carousel you actually published: link or screenshots, its date and channel.
6. **The verification trail (Lesson 3.2)** — brief, AI draft, claim-by-claim marks (✓ / ✗ / ?), final edit. Show the marks — this is the graded centrepiece.
7. **Copy set (Lesson 3.3)** — four hooks, benefit ladders, the three headline variants, the single CTA.
8. **The waterfall (Lesson 3.4)** — at least three repurposed assets drafted from the published piece, plus the dated distribution checklist and your metrics/kill-criteria lines.
9. **Two-week readout** — whatever numbers exist after two weeks (views, saves, link clicks, enquiries), honestly reported, with one paragraph: what you would double down on and what you would stop.
10. **Reflection (250–300 words)** — where the workflow saved you, where verification caught something real, and what you would change at 10× scale.

## Coverage requirements

- The calendar must mix funnel stages (~60/30/10) and name the format for every piece.
- The published piece must carry an AI-use statement and a single CTA.
- The verification trail must show at least one claim cut or corrected — if nothing failed verification, your sources were too thin; add one risky claim and check it.
- The waterfall must include at least one channel you have never posted to before.

## Process (suggested)

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Week 3, session 2 | Strategy line, pillars, calendar draft; pick the piece to publish first. |
| Week 3, self-study | Full brief → draft → verify → edit → disclose cycle; publish. |
| Week 4, session 1 | Peer review: partner audits your verification trail and tries to find one claim you missed. |
| Week 4, self-study | Waterfall assets, distribution, two-week readout; submit. |

## Rubric (7 marks)

| Criterion | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy coherence** | Calendar without logic | Topics trace to research | One-line strategy; every piece traceable to keyword, persona or gap |
| **AI workflow** | Raw AI output submitted | Brief and edit present | Full trail with claim-by-claim marks and an AI-use statement |
| **Copy craft** | Features only | Benefits present | Hook variety, benefit ladders, one honest CTA, three usable variants |
| **Publishing reality** | Nothing published | Published somewhere | Published with distribution executed and screenshots of it live |
| **Waterfall** | One format only | Two assets | 3+ channel-native assets, all linked back, dated distribution plan |
| **Evidence readout** | No numbers | Numbers reported | Numbers + a clear double-down/stop decision with reasoning |
| **Sustainability** | 100% capacity plan | Cut order marked | 50% capacity, cut order, and a kill criterion stated in advance |
