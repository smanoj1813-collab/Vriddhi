# Capstone — Integrated Campaign Demo Day

> **Project plan** · 90 minutes guided + 8–10 hours independent work across Weeks 9–10 · Present on Demo Day · Weight: 30% of the final grade

## The brief

This is the course's proving ground. You will plan, launch, run and iterate a real integrated marketing campaign for a real audience — then pitch the results in five minutes, honestly, to a panel that will ask the questions from Lesson 8.3.

Choose one of:

- **A live small business** — a shop, clinic, tuition centre, café or creator you can actually access (a relative, a family friend, your own side hustle). Strongly preferred.
- **A genuine cause or community** — a college club, a local event, a temple festival, a fundraiser.
- **A documented practice client** — only if neither of the above is possible; every number must be labelled "practice" throughout.

The campaign must run **at least two weeks** and use **at least three channels** from the course: search/GBP/SEO, content/organic social, paid (any budget, including ₹0-boost-only if you disclose the constraint), email/WhatsApp lifecycle, or creators/UGC. One channel must be **owned** (email list or WhatsApp community) — rented reach alone fails the brief, as Module 7 taught.

Everything is due on Demo Day: the campaign pack and the five-minute pitch.

## What to submit

The campaign pack (one Notion/doc link, ordered exactly like this):

1. **The strategy one-pager:** business context, goal with a numeric target, audience persona, positioning message, the strategy one-liner (Lesson 5.1 format), KPI tree with CPA guardrail, and the 70/20/10 channel plan with reasoning.
2. **The channel assets:** the ad copy or promoted post set, the content calendar (2 weeks minimum), the landing page or enquiry form design, the email/WhatsApp sequence (min. 3 messages), and any creator/UGC brief.
3. **The consent layer:** signup/consent copy, the privacy line, and the unsubscribe/exit path — per Lesson 7.4.
4. **The measurement stack:** GA4 (or platform analytics) event plan, the five-panel dashboard or sheet, and the how-did-you-hear log design.
5. **The campaign journal:** weekly entries across the run — what launched, what the numbers said (the weekly ritual), the ONE decision each entry, and its outcome. Minimum three entries.
6. **The iteration record:** at least one A/B test or experiment — hypothesis, method, sample, result, decision — plus at least one documented kill/scale decision with its pre-set rule.
7. **The results pack:** final numbers against targets — leads/conversions, CPA, channel split, assisted/incrementality evidence, and what you would do with one more month and ₹25,000 more.
8. **The failure analysis:** the worst-performing element, diagnosed honestly using the four-name pattern (miss, diagnosis, change, transfer).
9. **The AI-use statement:** which tools helped at which stage, and what you verified by hand.
10. **The five-minute pitch:** slides or script for Demo Day — problem, strategy, execution, results (numbers on screen), failure, learnings. You will present live.

## Coverage requirements

The capstone must visibly integrate at least: one deliverable from each of Modules 1–3 (brief/KPI tree, SEO or listing work, content strategy), Module 4 or 5 (paid OR social/community execution), Module 6 (dashboard + experiment + insight ritual), and Module 7 (lifecycle sequence or CRM pipeline with consent). A campaign that is all launch and no measurement fails; a campaign with numbers but no consent layer fails. Integration IS the assessment.

## Process

| Week | Milestone | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 9 · Days 1–2 | Client + goal locked | One-pager drafted; baselines recorded BEFORE launch (Lesson 6.1) |
| 9 · Days 3–5 | Assets built | Calendar, copy, landing, emails, consent layer, measurement stack |
| 9 · Day 6 | Launch | Campaign live on all channels; week-1 ritual scheduled |
| 9 · Day 7 → 10 · Day 7 | Run + iterate | Three weekly rituals logged; one experiment run; kill/scale rules applied |
| 10 · Days 8–9 | Results + pack | Numbers pulled, results pack, failure analysis |
| 10 · Day 10 | Rehearse | Pitch timed aloud twice, recorded once (Lesson 8.3 protocol) |
| Demo Day | Present | 5 minutes + panel questions; portfolio link on screen |

## Rubric

| Criterion | 0–1 · Missing | 2 · Developing | 3 · Professional | 4 · Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy | No one-pager or vague goal | Goal numeric but audience/channel reasoning thin | Complete one-pager; one-liner, KPI tree and 70/20/10 all coherent | Strategy visibly shaped execution choices; trade-offs explained |
| Execution | Fewer than three channels or under two weeks | Three channels but asset quality uneven | Three channels, two weeks, assets consistent with brand-kit discipline | Channels reinforce each other; owned channel compounding |
| Measurement | Numbers missing or unaudited | Dashboard present, ritual inconsistent | Five-panel stack, three rituals logged, experiment documented | Incrementality evidence used to justify a reallocation decision |
| Lifecycle & consent | Absent | Sequence present, consent thin | Consent layer complete; sequence + pipeline active | A/B on the lifecycle; lost-reasons feeding a change |
| Honest reporting | Vanity metrics only | Numbers without baselines | Baselines, guards, labelled practice data | Failure analysis with the four-name pattern, unprompted depth |
| The pitch | Untimed or read from notes | Clear but overruns or lacks numbers | 5 minutes, numbers on screen, failure owned, questions handled | Panel-grade: calm under probe, invited follow-ups, memorable one-liner |
| AI use | No statement | Generic statement | Tools and uses specific; verification described | AI clearly multiplied output while judgment stayed human |

**Pass mark: professional (3) on at least 5 of 7 criteria, with no criterion at 0–1 on Strategy or Measurement.** The pitch itself must be delivered live — a submitted video only counts with prior arrangement.

**On Demo Day, the panel asks from Lesson 8.3's ten questions — bring your five STAR-R stories, your dashboard on a tab, and your portfolio.**
