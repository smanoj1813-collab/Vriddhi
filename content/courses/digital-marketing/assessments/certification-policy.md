# Certification Policy — Certified Digital Marketing Practitioner (Vriddhi)

Programme: **VDM-101 Digital Marketing — Certificate Program** · 52 hours · 4 credits (recommended) · 10 weeks · Version 1.0.0 (September 2026)

## 1. Award

The certificate **Certified Digital Marketing Practitioner (Vriddhi)** is awarded to learners who meet all of the following:

1. **Overall score ≥ 50%** across the weighted components (quizzes 15, project blocks 28, capstone 30, final assessment 20, journal 7).
2. **Capstone ≥ 12/30** (40% of its weight).
3. **At least 3 of 4 Project Blocks submitted** and marked (a submission scoring 0 for integrity reasons does not count as submitted).
4. **Attendance/activity ≥ 75%** of guided sessions for instructor-led batches; for self-paced learners, completion of all lesson quizzes and journal entries substitutes for attendance.
5. **No unresolved academic-integrity finding.**

Grade bands: **Distinction ≥ 80 · Merit ≥ 65 · Pass ≥ 50 · Not yet < 50.** The certificate states the grade band, the programme version and the date; a verification link or QR code points to the credential record.

### Platform completion gate

The course app applies a separate, visible eligibility gate before it creates the learner's completion record. A learner must (1) reach the end of every lesson and project brief, (2) submit every lesson quiz, (3) achieve a best-attempt lesson-quiz average of at least **60%**, and (4) pass all eight end-of-module mini-assessments at **60% or higher**. Each module mini-assessment is available only after all of that module's topics have been read and all lesson quizzes submitted; passing unlocks the next module. The app merges offline progress into the signed-in student's college-scoped record. This platform checklist does not independently verify attendance, project marking, final assessment, identity, or academic-integrity clearance; the facilitator/programme lead must still apply the award conditions above before recording an institutional award.

## 2. Credits and recognition

- Designed as a **4-credit** skill-enhancement / value-added course (52 notational hours: ~18 guided, ~17 self-study, ~17 lab/project) suitable for affiliated colleges' add-on course frameworks. Colleges map credits under their own regulations; the programme team provides the syllabus, hour split and assessment scheme on request.
- Hours may be recorded in the college's activity or ability-enhancement register where applicable.
- The certificate does not claim recognition by any statutory body unless stated on the certificate itself.

## 3. Academic integrity and AI use

The programme teaches professional AI-assisted marketing; the integrity rules follow from that.

**Expected:** using AI tools for drafting, keyword clustering, copy variants, data summaries and rehearsal, **disclosed** through the AI-use statement on every submission, with the learner's own selection, verification, measurement and judgement.

**Misconduct:**

- Submitting AI-generated work as one's own reasoning without disclosure; false or misleading AI-use statements.
- Fabricating campaign data, analytics screenshots, results, or client consent — including presenting simulated numbers as real.
- Reproducing lesson material, other learners' work or public material without attribution.
- Using AI tools during Part A of the final assessment; communicating with others during Part B.
- Ethical violations in project work: messaging real people without consent; scraped or bought email/WhatsApp lists; undisclosed paid creator promotions; publishing real customers' personal data without permission; deceptive urgency or claims in campaign copy.

**Process:** the facilitator documents the concern and evidence; the learner responds within 7 days; a panel of two (facilitator + programme lead or another facilitator) decides. **Outcomes** range from resubmission with a cap, to zero on the component, to withholding of the certificate. Decisions are recorded; learners may appeal once to the programme lead within 14 days.

## 4. Attempts, resits and resubmissions

| Situation | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Lesson quizzes | Unlimited attempts; best attempt counts. |
| Project block below 3.5/7 | One resubmission within 10 days of feedback; capped at 5/7. |
| Project block not submitted | May be submitted late per §5; with ≥ 3 blocks submitted, a missing fourth block scores 0 without preventing certification. |
| Capstone below 12/30 | One revised submission (campaign pack and recorded pitch) within 21 days; capped at 18/30. |
| Final assessment below 40% | One resit within 30 days; the resit mark is capped at 60% of the component. |
| Overall below 50 after all of the above | "Not yet" — learner may re-enrol in a later cohort; project block and capstone marks may be carried forward for 12 months. |

## 5. Deadlines, extensions and late work

- Deadlines are published in the cohort calendar; the default rhythm is: PB1 Week 3, PB2 Week 4, PB3 Week 6, PB4 Week 8, capstone campaign Weeks 9–10 with Demo Day in Week 10, final assessment Week 10.
- **Extensions** of up to 7 days are granted for documented illness, bereavement, exam clashes or connectivity failure; request before the deadline where possible.
- **Late without extension:** ≤ 3 days −1 mark; 4–10 days capped at 4/7 (capstone pack −4); > 10 days accepted for completion but scored 0 unless the programme lead approves otherwise.

## 6. Accessibility and inclusion

- Reasonable accommodations (extra time, readers/scribes, alternative formats, flexible deadlines) are provided on request with basic documentation; requests go to the facilitator or programme lead in Week 1 where possible.
- Learners may write journal entries, Part B of the final assessment and project reflections in **English, Kannada or Hindi**; other languages by arrangement.
- Free tiers of every tool are sufficient for all requirements (Google Business Profile, Search Console, GA4, Looker Studio, free email tiers); where a tool is unavailable or blocked, the facilitator approves an equivalent. No learner is penalised for a paid feature, and no campaign budget is required to pass — organic-only campaigns are fully acceptable with the constraint disclosed.
- Learners without a private device or reliable connectivity should be prioritised for lab time; hands-on labs may be completed on shared devices with the facilitator's record.

## 7. Data protection for learners and their audiences

- The programme collects the minimum personal data needed for enrolment, assessment and certification; progress data held in the Vriddhi app is described in the app's privacy notice.
- Capstone and project work involving real people (client lists, customer data, creator collaborations) must follow the course's DPDP-aligned consent checklists from Lesson 7.4; facilitators may require anonymisation before marking.
- Campaigns that message real people require documented consent or a legitimate existing relationship; the course provides templates.
- Learner submissions may be used as anonymised exemplars for training facilitators only with the learner's written consent.

## 8. Quality assurance and versioning

- The course pack is versioned (`course.json` → `version`, `lastReviewed`). Cohorts are assessed against the version they enrolled on.
- Each cohort: moderation per `rubrics.md` §5; item statistics for the final assessment; learner feedback survey; a platform-landscape check (ad platforms, GA4, mailbox sender rules, ASCI guidelines) and a legal-update check (DPDP Rules commencement stages) before the next cohort.
- Material changes to assessment weights or pass conditions are announced before a cohort starts, never during it.

## 9. Certificate verification and revocation

- Certificates carry a unique ID; verification is available through the Vriddhi platform.
- A certificate may be revoked if a serious integrity finding is established after award; the learner is notified with reasons and has the right of appeal in §3.

## 10. Roles

- **Facilitator:** delivers sessions, supports labs, marks, records attendance, raises integrity concerns.
- **Second marker / moderator:** samples and moderates per `rubrics.md` §5.
- **Programme lead:** owns this policy, decides appeals, approves extensions beyond 7 days and equivalent-tool substitutions, maintains the held-back item bank.
- **Learner:** engages honestly, discloses AI use, protects other people's data, meets deadlines or asks early.

*Questions about this policy go to the programme lead named in the cohort calendar.*
