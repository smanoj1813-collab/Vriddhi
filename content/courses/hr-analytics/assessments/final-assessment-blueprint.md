# Final assessment blueprint — VHR-101 HR Analytics

## 1. Format and conditions

Duration: 90 minutes, scheduled separately from the 60 learning hours. Part A: 40 MCQs, 45 minutes, 1.5 raw marks each (60 marks). Part B: two applied cases, 45 minutes total, 20 raw marks each (40 marks). Raw total 100, scaled to 20 overall marks. No negative marking. Offer approved accessibility accommodations without changing the learning outcomes.

The final is closed-AI and has no internet or messaging. Part A is closed-notes. Part B permits the supplied case sheet, a basic calculator and the facilitator-designated printed glossary. Project AI permissions do not carry into the final. Publish these conditions and the assessment date at cohort start.

## 2. Coverage matrix

| Module | MCQs | Illustrative skill tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. People analytics foundations and responsible questions | 5 | Connect recruitment and workforce measures to a practical decision without treating people as scores. |
| 2. Data preparation and spreadsheet analysis | 5 | Preserve raw data, identify record keys and document cleaning decisions before calculating HR metrics. |
| 3. Recruitment funnel and selection analytics | 5 | Calculate stage conversion and compare candidate sources using consistent cohorts. |
| 4. Workforce planning and core HR metrics | 5 | Calculate headcount and full-time equivalents and reconcile workforce movements at the correct boundary. |
| 5. Engagement, retention and development | 5 | Design a concise survey and interpret response distributions with confidentiality and nonresponse limits. |
| 6. Dashboards and HR storytelling | 5 | Choose dashboard measures and visual encodings based on a decision, not on the number of available fields. |
| 7. Responsible people analytics and evaluation | 5 | Recognise alternative explanations and design a more credible evaluation of an HR intervention. |
| 8. HR analytics capstone and career launch | 5 | Assemble a reproducible case study with a data dictionary, quality log, dashboard and bounded recommendation. |

Each module contributes five MCQs: two concept/interpretation items, two applied scenario items and one calculation or evidence-quality item. Across the paper use 16 core and 24 applied/advanced items. Include plausible misconceptions rather than trick wording. Do not require a particular paid tool, memorised menu position or unsupported current legal claim.

## 3. Public practice cases

These cases and checkpoints are public preparation material, not secret final-exam questions.

### Case A — planning and evidence

A mature recruitment cohort contains 200 applications, 40 interviews, 20 offers, 15 acceptances and 12 joins. Allocated recruitment cost is ₹120,000. A recent comparison cohort still has open cases. Prepare a recruitment performance and data-quality note without treating source differences as causal.

Required response: define the population or scope; calculate the relevant measures with units; explain a limitation or alternative interpretation; compare actions; recommend a next step with owner and approval/review point.

**Practice checkpoint:** Interview-to-offer 50%; offer acceptance 75%; application-to-join 6%; cost per joined hire ₹10,000. State matched cohorts and observation windows; recent open cases cannot be treated as completed outcomes.

### Case B — quality, safeguards and action

Opening headcount is 100, closing headcount 120, exits 11, and 92 original employees remain. A survey receives 48 of 80 replies, of which 30 are favourable. A dashboard exposes a four-person subgroup, and a vendor claims 90% exit-model accuracy where 90% of employees stay. Prepare a responsible HR decision memo.

Required response: identify the misleading or unsafe claims; show the necessary calculations; state what the evidence does and does not establish; propose a proportionate corrective action with an accountable role and review measure.

**Practice checkpoint:** Average-headcount turnover 10%; opening-cohort retention 92%; response 60%; favourable among respondents 62.5%. Apply disclosure safeguards and note that an all-stay baseline also has 90% accuracy but zero exit recall.

## 4. Case rubric — 20 raw marks per case

| Criterion | Marks | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definition and scope | 4 | Correct population, period, boundary or baseline; assumptions stated |
| Method and calculation | 6 | Correct formulas/reasoning, units and reproducible working |
| Interpretation and limitations | 5 | Correct meaning, uncertainty and no unsupported causal or authority claim |
| Recommendation and safeguards | 5 | Feasible action, owner, review trigger and appropriate controls |

Award method credit for transparent working even if a minor arithmetic slip occurs. Do not award a full recommendation score where the action depends on an uncorrected material error or unsafe data use. Equivalent offline methods are valid.

## 5. Facilitator paper preparation

The bundled lesson and module banks contain visible explanations and are deliberately public. They are not a secure item bank. Before each cohort’s final, prepare a separate unseen paper following the matrix, using new but comparable scenarios and values; retain it and its key only in approved staff storage, never in the client bundle or public repository. This blueprint supplies the specification and practice cases, not a claim that a secure examination service is implemented.

A second facilitator independently checks every answer, distractor, calculation and case rubric. Test that each MCQ has one defensible best answer under its stated assumptions. Avoid repeating a public question verbatim as evidence of unseen performance. Prepare an equivalent resit paper before release of final feedback. Provide all necessary data in the paper so a learner is not assessed on personal access to a platform.

## 6. Administration and review

Verify learner identity through the institution’s approved process, explain permitted materials, record accommodations and collect scripts securely. Review missing responses and ambiguous items before finalising marks. If an item is defective, apply a documented cohort-wide correction rather than selectively helping particular learners. Moderate borderline awards and retain the paper version, key, marks and decisions under institutional policy. Do not deploy or modify Firestore rules for this content assessment workflow.
