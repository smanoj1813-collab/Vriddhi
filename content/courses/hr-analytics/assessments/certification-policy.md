# Certification policy — VHR-101 HR Analytics

Version 1.0.0 · Pack date 2026-09-28 · Cohorts use the version published at enrolment.

## 1. Learning scope and recognition

The course contains 60 learning hours over ten weeks: 24 lessons of 90 minutes (36 hours), four project blocks of 180 minutes (12 hours), and a 720-minute capstone (12 hours). Lesson time includes guided reading, worked examples, labs, quizzes and reflection. Module mini-assessments are scheduled within the module review/quiz allocation. The 90-minute institutional final assessment is scheduled separately from these 60 learning hours; do not double-count project work already included in a lesson lab.

The manifest proposes four credits for local curriculum mapping. Credit recognition is subject to the college’s regulations; the pack does not itself grant statutory accreditation, professional-body membership or external vendor certification. Completion does not guarantee employment. The course prepares portfolio evidence relevant to HR Executive, HR Analyst, Talent Acquisition Associate roles.

## 2. Assessment scheme

| Component | Overall marks | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson quizzes | 15 | Mean best-attempt percentage across all 24 lessons × 0.15 |
| Project Blocks 1–4 | 28 | Four facilitator-marked blocks, each out of 7 |
| Capstone | 30 | Published rubric, presentation and individual defence |
| Final assessment | 20 | Raw score out of 100 × 0.20 |
| Reflection journal | 7 | Evidence, reasoning and learning across modules |
| Total | 100 | Sum of the five component marks |

A missing component receives zero unless an approved extension or accommodation applies. Do not average only completed lesson quizzes for the institutional quiz component; unsubmitted lessons contribute zero until completed. Mini-assessments are platform progression gates and do not create a sixth weighted component.

## 3. Institutional award and grade bands

The institutional award requires an overall score of at least 50/100, capstone at least 12/30, submission of at least three of the four project blocks, at least 75% attendance in instructor-led cohorts, and academic-integrity clearance. The programme lead verifies these conditions from marked work and institutional records.

| Band | Overall percentage |
| --- | --- |
| Distinction | 80 or above |
| Merit | 65 to below 80 |
| Pass | 50 to below 65 |
| Not yet | Below 50 |

Bands describe the numeric overall score. A high overall band does not override a failed award condition, such as a capstone below 12. Record “award pending/not yet eligible” separately when necessary. Use unrounded overall values for threshold comparisons; round only the displayed score consistently to one decimal place.

## 4. Platform completion versus institutional award

The app’s platform eligibility checks require reading every lesson and project brief, submitting every lesson quiz, reaching a best-attempt lesson-quiz average of at least 60%, and passing all eight module assessments at 60% or higher. Each module has ten advanced scenario questions; six correct meet the gate. Reading all module topics and submitting its lesson quizzes unlock its assessment; passing unlocks the next module.

Reading a project brief is not proof that a project was submitted or passed. The bundled app does not automatically mark project files, verify attendance, conduct the institutional final assessment or clear integrity concerns. Its platform completion certificate/checklist must not be presented as independent verification of those institutional award conditions. Facilitators keep external marks and award decisions in approved college records.

## 5. Attempts and resubmissions

Lesson quizzes and module assessments permit repeat practice; the best recorded result counts for their respective purposes. Feedback and explanations are visible, so these banks are learning resources, not secret high-stakes exam material.

One project-block resubmission is allowed within ten days of feedback, capped at 5/7. One capstone revision is allowed within twenty-one days, capped at 18/30. One final resit is allowed within thirty days, capped at 60/100 raw (12/20 weighted). Approved accommodations or documented marking errors are handled by the programme lead and are not automatically penalised under these caps. A failed overall award may be reconsidered after eligible resubmissions; record both attempts and the applicable rule.

## 6. Deadlines, accessibility and language

Publish due dates and the final-assessment date before the cohort starts. Project blocks follow their associated modules; the capstone occupies the closing two weeks with preparation beginning earlier. Request extensions before the deadline where possible. The programme lead may grant a seven-day extension for illness, examination clashes, bereavement or connectivity barriers and document longer exceptions when justified.

Late work without an approved extension is accepted within ten days with a cap of 5/7 for a block or 24/30 for a capstone; beyond that, it is recorded for completion but earns zero unless the programme lead approves an exception. Where multiple caps apply, use the lower cap and explain it to the learner in advance.

Provide reasonable accommodations, accessible materials, shared-lab time and offline-equivalent tools. Learners may write reflections and present in English, Kannada or Hindi by arrangement; technical terms, formulas and evidence must remain clear. No learner is penalised for not owning a paid software subscription.

## 7. Integrity, data and AI use

Use synthetic data throughout. Do not upload identifiable candidate, employee, student, customer or supplier records to public tools. Do not infer sensitive attributes or fabricate approval, business outcomes, source references or employment experience. Disclose AI assistance on every project, including “none” where appropriate. Verify all AI-assisted calculations and claims independently. Public quiz explanations may be studied but must not be represented as unseen examination performance.

The final assessment is closed-AI throughout. Part A is closed-notes; Part B permits the supplied case sheet, a basic calculator and the printed metric/formula glossary designated by the facilitator. No internet, messaging or external assistance is allowed unless explicitly approved as an accommodation.

For an integrity concern, the facilitator records evidence and gives the learner seven days to respond. A second reviewer and programme lead decide proportionate action, such as correction, resubmission, component zero or withholding the award. The learner may appeal once within fourteen days to an authorised reviewer not responsible for the original decision. Never publish allegations or private records in a class group.

## 8. Quality assurance and review

Moderate all borderline award cases and a sample of at least 20% of other projects, including high and low scores. Review ambiguous quiz items and correct errors transparently without disadvantaging learners. Preserve cohort versions and decision records under institutional retention policy. Before each new cohort, verify tool access, external guidance and applicable privacy/employment requirements; the pack date is not a claim of independent legal or professional accreditation review.
