# Portfolio templates — VPM-101

Use these as editable starting points, not as forms that automatically prove good practice. Complete only fields relevant to the brief and explain omissions. All entries must be synthetic or appropriately aggregate; no real personal data, credentials or confidential contracts belong in a public portfolio.

## Work, risk and change registers

```text
Work: ID | deliverable | owner role | effort | duration | predecessors | acceptance | status | evidence

```

## Portfolio story and interview prompt

Write a one-page story: decision/problem → constraints → method → evidence → result → limitation → learning. Label the work as a course simulation. Rehearse explaining a correction and reproducing a central value. Do not claim live business impact, external credentials or employment experience that the evidence does not support.
